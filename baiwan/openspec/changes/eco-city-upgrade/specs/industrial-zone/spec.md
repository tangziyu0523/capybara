## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sun Valley Zone
The system SHALL include a dedicated Industrial Zone named "Sun Valley".

#### Scenario: Industrial Aesthetics
- **WHEN** the Sun Valley district is generated
- **THEN** buildings resemble factories, warehouses, or solar plants
- **AND** they are arranged in a functional, organized manner

#### Scenario: Spatial Allocation
- **WHEN** the city is laid out
- **THEN** Sun Valley occupies approx 20% of the area
- **AND** is located in the South sector
