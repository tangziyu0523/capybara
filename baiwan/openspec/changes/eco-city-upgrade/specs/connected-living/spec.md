## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Visual Corridors
The system SHALL maintain clear lines of sight between specific zones.

#### Scenario: Forest-Tidal Sightline
- **WHEN** Forest Home is placed
- **THEN** there is an unobstructed visual path (approx 50-80m wide) towards the Tidal Lounge/Ocean

### Requirement: Landscape Transition
The system SHALL render vegetation belts between zones.

#### Scenario: Green Belt
- **WHEN** zones border each other
- **THEN** a dense strip of vegetation (15m wide) separates them
