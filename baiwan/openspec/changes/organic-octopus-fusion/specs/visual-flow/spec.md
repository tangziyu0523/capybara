## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Fluid Transportation
The system SHALL render transportation paths as organic curves following the structural arms.

#### Scenario: Flow Lines
- **WHEN** tubes are generated
- **THEN** they follow the curvature of the terrain arms
- **AND** avoid straight line segments or sharp corners

### Requirement: Dynamic Form
The system SHALL present a visually dynamic, flowing silhouette.

#### Scenario: View from Above
- **WHEN** viewed from a high angle
- **THEN** the city resembles an organic form (like an octopus or neuron) rather than a geometric shape
