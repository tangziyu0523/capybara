## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Canyon-Centric Layout
The system SHALL organize the terrain and districts around a central winding canyon.

#### Scenario: The Sun Canyon
- **WHEN** terrain is generated
- **THEN** a depression (canyon) is sculpted along a curved path through the island center
- **AND** the terrain color in the canyon is distinct (warm earth tones)

### Requirement: Biome-Based Placement
The system SHALL place districts according to their "biome" affinity.

#### Scenario: District Zoning
- **WHEN** districts are placed
- **THEN** "Sun Valley" is located within the Canyon floor
- **AND** "Forest Home" is located on the higher green plateaus
- **AND** "Coral Reef Street" bridges or lines the Canyon walls
