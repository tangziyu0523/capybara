## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Style Differentiation
The system SHALL render distinct architectural styles for each zone.

#### Scenario: Organic Tidal Style
- **WHEN** Tidal Lounge buildings are generated
- **THEN** they use curved/spherical geometries (biomorphic)
- **AND** avoid sharp boxy corners

#### Scenario: Eco Forest Style
- **WHEN** Forest Home buildings are generated
- **THEN** they use wooden/natural materials
- **AND** feature stilted or tree-house like structures

#### Scenario: Visual Coherence
- **WHEN** the scene is rendered
- **THEN** all zones share a common lighting/atmosphere to ensure they feel like one city
