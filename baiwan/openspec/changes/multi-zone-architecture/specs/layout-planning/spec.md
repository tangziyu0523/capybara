## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Zoned Layout
The system SHALL arrange districts according to the specific functional zones: Coastal, Central, Inland.

#### Scenario: Tidal Placement
- **WHEN** Tidal Lounge is placed
- **THEN** it is located at the edge of the island (East)
- **AND** its elevation is near water level

#### Scenario: Forest Placement
- **WHEN** Forest Home is placed
- **THEN** it is located deep inland (West)
- **AND** it is surrounded by vegetation

### Requirement: Central Connection
The system SHALL feature a dual-core center.

#### Scenario: Twin Cores
- **WHEN** the Central District is generated
- **THEN** it contains two distinct large-volume structures
- **AND** they are visually connected (e.g., by proximity or a bridge structure)
