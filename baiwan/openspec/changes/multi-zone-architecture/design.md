# Design: Tri-Zone Eco-Architecture

## 1. Layout Strategy (Ref Fig 5)
The island is divided into 3 distinct zones:
- **Coast (East)**: Low elevation, sandy. Home to **Tidal Lounge**.
- **Center (Mid)**: Mid elevation. Home to **Central Core**.
- **Inland (West)**: High elevation, forested. Home to **Forest Home**.

### Spatial Relations
- **Tidal Lounge**: $x > 150$, $y \approx 0$ (Water level).
- **Central Core**: $x \approx 0$, $y \approx 30$.
- **Forest Home**: $x < -100$, $y > 50$ (Hilltop).

## 2. Architectural Styles

### A. Central Core (Dual Towers)
- **Style**: Modern/Futurist.
- **Form**: Two dominant vertical structures connected by a bridge or plaza.
- **Material**: Glass/Steel (Blue/White).

### B. Tidal Lounge (Ref Fig 3)
- **Style**: Organic/Biomorphic.
- **Form**: Flattened spheres, domes, or wave-like roofs. Large glass facades facing the ocean.
- **Material**: Metallic Blue, Glass, White concrete.
- **Constraint**: Must look "floating" or amphibious.

### C. Forest Home (Ref Fig 4)
- **Style**: Eco-Cabin.
- **Form**: Small footprint, vertical integration with trees (stilts), pitched or green roofs.
- **Material**: Wood textures, Green roofs.

## 3. Traffic & Flow
- A primary "Artery" connects Tidal -> Center -> Forest.
- Secondary paths wind through the forest and along the coast.
