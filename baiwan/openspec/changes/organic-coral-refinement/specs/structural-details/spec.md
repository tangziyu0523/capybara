## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Functional Structures
The system SHALL render specific functional sub-structures.

#### Scenario: Honeycomb Base
- **WHEN** Tidal Lounge is generated
- **THEN** it sits on a hexagonal grid platform

#### Scenario: Wind Turbines
- **WHEN** Sun Valley is generated
- **THEN** helical wind turbines are visible on tower tops

### Requirement: Integrated Transport
The system SHALL intertwine transport with architecture.

#### Scenario: Building Penetration
- **WHEN** Maglev tubes are generated
- **THEN** they pass through the bounding box of the Industrial Zone (Sun Valley)
- **AND** appear to connect to the buildings
