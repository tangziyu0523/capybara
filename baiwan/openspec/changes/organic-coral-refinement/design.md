# Design: Nature-Tech Coral City

## 1. Visual Refinement
### A. Tidal Lounge (Transparent Coral)
- **Material**: `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with `transmission: 0.9`, `roughness: 0.05`, `ior: 1.5` (Glass).
- **Form**: "Polyp" clusters. A central clear dome surrounded by smaller colorful/glowing nodes.
- **Base**: Hexagonal prism grid (Honeycomb) clearly visible at water level.

### B. Forest Home (Wood & Bamboo)
- **Material**: `MeshStandardMaterial` with a custom color to simulate "Wood Grain" (Brown/Ochre variation).
- **Form**: "Tree Houses". Elevated on thin struts (Bamboo). Roofs are saddle-shaped (Hyperbolic Paraboloid).

### C. Sun Valley (Tech-Ecology)
- **Vertical Farm**: Glass cylinders with internal green helical structures (Aeroponics).
- **Wind Turbine**: Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (VAWT) - Helix shape spinning on top.

## 2. Integrated Systems
- **Maglev**: The tube path will be adjusted to pass *through* the gaps in the Sun Valley towers and wrap *around* the Central Core like a vine.
- **Logistics**: Visual indication of "pipes" connecting farms to the ground.

## 3. Lighting & Atmosphere
- Increase ambient light to show off the transparency.
- Add a soft "glow" to the Tidal zone (Emissive materials).
