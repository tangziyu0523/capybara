## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Inter-District Connectivity
The system SHALL render visual connections between the central hub and peripheral districts.

#### Scenario: Transportation Tubes
- **WHEN** the scene is rendered
- **THEN** 3D tubes or paths connect the center of Coral Reef Street to the centers of other districts
- **AND** the paths follow a smooth curve (spline)

#### Scenario: Traffic Animation
- **WHEN** the scene is active
- **THEN** light pulses or particles travel along the transportation paths to simulate movement
