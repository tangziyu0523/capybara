## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Integrated Maglev
The system SHALL render transportation tracks that physically interact with building structures.

#### Scenario: Building Penetration/Wrap
- **WHEN** Maglev tracks are generated
- **THEN** they pass through or wrap closely around major building clusters (especially in the Core and Sun Valley)
- **AND** stations are visually integrated into the building mass

#### Scenario: Underground Logistics
- **WHEN** (Implicit)
- **THEN** (Visual) Vertical farm bases show connections to "underground" hubs (e.g., pipes entering the ground)
