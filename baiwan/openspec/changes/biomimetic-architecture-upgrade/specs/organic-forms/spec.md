## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Organic Geometry
The system SHALL generate buildings with fluid, non-rectilinear shapes.

#### Scenario: Coral Forms
- **WHEN** Tidal Lounge buildings are generated
- **THEN** they are composed of spherical or curved clusters
- **AND** they avoid sharp 90-degree angles

#### Scenario: Curved Roofs
- **WHEN** Forest Home buildings are generated
- **THEN** roofs are rendered as curved surfaces (vaults or domes)
- **AND** structural supports resemble organic branches (bamboo/wood)
