## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Nature-Tech Materials
The system SHALL use PBR materials that simulate natural and advanced composite materials.

#### Scenario: Engineered Wood
- **WHEN** Forest Home is rendered
- **THEN** materials simulate Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT) or Bamboo
- **AND** surfaces have appropriate roughness/normal maps (simulated)

#### Scenario: Smart Glass
- **WHEN** Tidal Lounge is rendered
- **THEN** domes use high-transmission glass materials
- **AND** internal "ocean energy" glow effects are visible

### Requirement: Industrial Ecology
The system SHALL render eco-industrial features in Sun Valley.

#### Scenario: Vertical Farming
- **WHEN** Sun Valley is rendered
- **THEN** towers feature transparent sections showing internal green "shelves"
- **AND** wind turbine structures are visible on rooftops
