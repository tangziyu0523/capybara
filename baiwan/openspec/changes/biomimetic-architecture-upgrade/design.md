# Design: Biomimetic Organic City

## 1. Architectural Morphology: "Coral Growth"
Instead of boxy extrusions, buildings will be generated using **Cluster Algorithms**.

### A. Tidal Lounge (Floating Coral)
- **Base**: Hexagonal/Honeycomb pontoon lattice.
- **Body**: Clusters of transparent domes (Hemispheres) and organic "tentacles" (Curved tubes).
- **Material**: Glass (85% transmission), White Bioplastic.

### B. Forest Home (Organic Cabins)
- **Structure**: "Tree-hugging" forms. Cylindrical cores with curved wooden shells (sections of spheres/cylinders).
- **Roof**: 3D Hyperbolic Paraboloid (saddle shape) or simple curved vaulted roofs using `TubeGeometry` or bent planes.
- **Material**: CLT Wood textures, Bamboo struts.

### C. Sun Valley (Eco-Industry)
- **Vertical Farms**: Stacked cylindrical glass towers with internal green spirals.
- **Wind Turbines**: Vertical axis turbines (helical shapes) integrated into tower tops.
- **Platforms**: Terraced layers (1.5-3m steps) following terrain contours.

## 2. Integrated Maglev System
- **Tracks**: Not just on the ground, but elevated "Ribbons" that wrap around the Central Core and Sun Valley.
- **Stations**: Integrated into the 2nd or 3rd floor of major buildings.

## 3. Materials & Visuals
- **PBR**: High fidelity.
- **Wood**: Roughness map to simulate grain.
- **Glass**: Physical transmission, clearcoat.
