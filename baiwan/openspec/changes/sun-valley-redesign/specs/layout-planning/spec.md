## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Sun Valley Layout
The system SHALL arrange the Sun Valley district in a linear, canyon-oriented layout.

#### Scenario: Canyon Spine
- **WHEN** Sun Valley buildings are generated
- **THEN** they are distributed along the primary North-South axis (Z-axis)
- **AND** a clear central corridor is preserved for transportation

#### Scenario: Terraced Density
- **WHEN** buildings are placed on the "valley walls"
- **THEN** they are spaced apart to allow visibility through the canyon
- **AND** the overall density is low (dispersed)

### Requirement: Integrated Transport Spine
The system SHALL align the transportation network with the architectural spine.

#### Scenario: Path Alignment
- **WHEN** the Sun Valley transport line is generated
- **THEN** it runs directly above the central building spine
- **AND** connects efficiently to the city core
